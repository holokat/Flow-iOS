## nspam v2.4

Halo bundles the nspam v2.4 reply-spam model and calibration artifacts from
https://huggingface.co/barrydeen/nspam/tree/abd4b0311891d6c5f088c2b5b7e4b9dc1bbbe303/v2.4.

The model was trained from nspam corpus v2.4, published at
https://huggingface.co/datasets/barrydeen/nspam-corpus/tree/707e4c12bf2a46d0dda71eca59be59a4456d630d/v2.4.
